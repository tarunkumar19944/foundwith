Unknown number calling? WhoHas shows which of your people have that number, so you can ask them who it is.

For any phone number, WhoHas shows how many people on WhoHas have it in their contacts or call log, and which of them you know. People you know are shown by name; everyone else is just a count.

WHAT YOU GET
• Right after a call: if someone on WhoHas has the caller's number, a notification tells you who you know has it. Copy the number, check it in Truecaller, or call back in one tap.
• Search any number: see how many people have it and ask someone you know on WhatsApp.
• Updates: every number on your phone that people on WhoHas have, with what changed on top.
• Your own number: see how many people have it, how close you are to the next level, and when a friend joins with your link.

YOUR PRIVACY
• WhoHas never messages or calls anyone on its own, never reads your messages and never listens to or records calls.
• Phone numbers are stored only as one-way codes, on servers in India. The names in your contacts stay on your phone.
• Your name is shown only to people who already have your number: as someone they know, or when they look up your number.
• Turn off contacts or call log any time, download your data, or delete your account in the app.

WhoHas works best when your friends and family use it too: share it in your group, and everyone can recognise more callers.

Sign in with an Indian mobile number. Every kind of number is checked: mobiles, landlines, toll-free, service numbers and numbers from abroad. For people aged 18 and over.
